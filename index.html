chanceify™

music reimagined .

A small Spotify player for your desktop. It plays your music, counts what you listen to, and keeps everything it saves in one folder next to the app.

  • Needs Spotify Premium
  • Music stats for 7 days, 30 days or all time
  • Last.fm scrobbling
  • 16 themes, or pick your own colours
  • No tracking. Free and open source (AGPL-3.0)
